**Koha – Resources, Configurations, and Documentation**

This repository aims to collect, organize, and share files, configurations, templates, and technical documentation related to Koha, an open-source Integrated Library System (ILS).

The content provided is based on practical experience with the software, particularly in cataloging, information organization and representation, metadata management, and system configuration.

This repository seeks to contribute to the documentation of technical procedures, knowledge sharing, and the dissemination of best practices among librarians, catalogers, system administrators, and other professionals interested in Koha.

**Objectives**

Share MARC 21 cataloging files and templates.

Provide configurations and customizations used in Koha.

Document technical and operational procedures.

Gather supporting materials for cataloging and managing bibliographic and authority records.

Promote the exchange of experiences and solutions related to the use of Koha.

Contribute to the standardization of workflows and the interoperability of bibliographic data.

**Repository Contents**

The repository may include the following resources:

**1. MARC 21**

Files, templates, and examples related to the use of the MARC 21 format in Koha, including:

Bibliographic records.

Authority records.

Holdings and item records.

MARC bibliographic frameworks.

Examples of fields and subfields.

Record import and export files.

**2. Koha Configurations**

Files and guidelines related to system configuration, such as:

System preferences.

Item types and patron categories.

Circulation rules.

Cataloging configurations.

Authorized values.

Authority configurations.

**3. SQL Reports and Queries**

SQL queries and reports developed to support library management activities and data analysis within Koha.

**4. Technical Documentation**

Manuals, tutorials, guidelines, and procedures related to the use and administration of the software.

**5. Additional Resources**

Other files and materials considered relevant to information organization, representation, retrieval, and management in libraries.

Repository Organization

Materials will be organized into thematic directories according to their nature and purpose to facilitate identification, access, and reuse.

Whenever possible, files will include descriptions of their purpose, application, requirements, and any specific considerations regarding their use.

Usage and Recommendations

The materials available in this repository are intended for technical, educational, and knowledge-sharing purposes.

Before using any file or configuration in a production environment, it is recommended to:

Verify compatibility with the Koha version in use.

Assess potential impacts on existing configurations.

Perform tests in a development or staging environment.

Maintain backups of data and system configurations.

The resources published here do not replace the official Koha documentation or the recommendations provided by the software maintainers.
